# Altvia

> Altvia is fund management software purpose-built for private capital markets — private equity, venture capital, fund of funds, real estate, hedge funds, family offices, and placement agents. Built on the Salesforce platform, it unifies CRM, a virtual data room (VDR) and LP investor portal, analytics/reporting, and an AI assistant (AIMe) into one system spanning fundraising, investor relations, and deal management. Altvia has no public developer/API portal; integrations are delivered through the platform and Salesforce ecosystem.
